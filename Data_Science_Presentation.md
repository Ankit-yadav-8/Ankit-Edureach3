## Slide 2: The Core Vision

---

## Slide 3: Key Feature - Three-Layer Mentor Dashboard System
*A hierarchical, data-driven visualization system tailored for different stakeholders.*

### Layer 1: Super Admin / Management View
- **Focus:** Macro-level platform health and analytics.
- **Features:** Platform-wide student success rates, mentor performance distributions, and resource allocation metrics.
- **Impact:** Enables leadership to make strategic decisions and maintain quality control across all mentors.

### Layer 2: Mentor View
- **Focus:** Cohort management and personalized guidance.
- **Features:** Mentee progress tracking, engagement scores, bottleneck identification, and automated alerts for students falling behind.
- **Impact:** Empowers mentors to focus their time where it is needed most, allowing them to handle larger cohorts efficiently.

### Layer 3: Student / Mentee View
- **Focus:** Motivation and milestone tracking.
- **Features:** Personal performance metrics, score trajectories, and anonymized peer benchmarking.
- **Impact:** Keeps students engaged, highly motivated, and clearly aware of their daily progress and next steps.

## Slide 4: Data Strategy & Execution
*How I will turn raw data into actionable insights.*

- **Data Pipeline Engineering:** Cleaning and standardizing data from mock tests, video watch times, and doubt resolutions.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Using Python (Pandas, NumPy) and SQL to identify correlations between study habits and exam success.
- **A/B Testing:** Designing experiments to see which mentorship styles or study materials yield the best retention rates.
