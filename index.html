CollegeParichay — JEE Rank Predictor and College Reviews by IIT Roorkee Alumni

CollegeParichay (collegeparichay.in) is a private student platform founded by two IIT Roorkee alumni — Ankit Yadav (Founder & CEO) and Ankit Kumar (Co-Founder & CTO). It started in an IIT Roorkee hostel room and is now headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan. CollegeParichay is an independent student startup — it is NOT the Rajasthan government Department of College Education (DCE) portal (hte.rajasthan.gov.in), and is not affiliated with any other platform of a similar name.

CollegeParichay provides: free JEE Main rank predictor, free JEE Advanced rank predictor, personalised college shortlist tool (College For You), IIT cutoffs, NIT cutoffs, IIIT cutoffs, BITS Pilani cutoffs, JoSAA counselling guide, CSAB counselling guide, verified engineering college reviews, placement statistics, and hostel reviews.
